## Reading note

Kalffian sandplay is a Jungian approach involving sand, water, miniatures and a protected therapeutic relationship. Broader sandtray practices may use different theories or directives; their methods and research should not be treated as interchangeable.

卡尔夫沙盘游戏属于荣格取向，在受保护的治疗关系中使用沙、水和微缩物。其他沙盘方法可能采用不同理论或指导方式，不能把各自的方法与研究视为完全相同。
