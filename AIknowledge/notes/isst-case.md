## Reading note

Document the construction sequence, sand use, placements and the creator’s words, alongside relevant context and changes across sessions. Keep recorded events distinct from the therapist’s reflections. A finished-tray image cannot supply missing process or history.

记录制作顺序、用沙方式、摆放、创作者原话，以及相关背景和跨次变化。把实际事件与治疗师的思考分开。完成后的沙盘照片不能补足缺失的过程或历史。
