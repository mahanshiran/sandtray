## Reading note

School group sandplay study with 113 children and ten sessions, assessed before and after the program. Without a randomized control comparison, changes do not isolate an intervention effect; avoid a universal session prescription.

113名儿童参加十次学校团体沙盘的前后测研究。缺少随机对照时，变化不能单独归因为干预；不应据此制定通用次数处方。
