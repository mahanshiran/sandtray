## Reading note

Qualitative longitudinal study of seven children, using trays at three stages of psychotherapy. Useful for studying change in context and over time; it cannot establish that any tray feature identifies abuse or that the intervention caused improvement.

对七名儿童在心理治疗三个阶段的沙盘进行质性纵向研究，可帮助学习结合情境观察变化；不能据沙盘特征判断受虐，也不能证明干预造成改善。
