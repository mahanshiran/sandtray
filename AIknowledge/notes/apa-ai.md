## Reading note

Professional AI use requires attention to transparency, privacy, bias, evidence and human oversight. A model-generated reflection needs professional review; an apparently fluent answer does not establish clinical validity.

专业使用AI应注意透明度、隐私、偏差、证据与人工监督。模型生成的反思需要专业审阅，语言流畅并不能证明临床有效性。
