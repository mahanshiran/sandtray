## Reading note

The builder determines personal meaning. Follow their language and pace; use open invitations instead of supplying a symbol interpretation. Avoid entering or changing the tray without invitation. Training and consultation support competent practice.

沙盘创作者决定其个人意义。跟随其语言与节奏，以开放邀请代替替对方解释象征。未经邀请不进入或改变沙盘。专业训练与督导支持胜任实践。
