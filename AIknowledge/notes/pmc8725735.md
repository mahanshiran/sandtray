## Reading note

Sixty college students participated in a restricted group sandplay experiment. Turn-taking and communication restrictions are specific to that protocol; findings cannot be assumed to apply to individual nondirective sandplay.

六十名大学生参加限制性团体沙盘实验。轮流制作与交流限制属于该方案，结果不能直接套用于个人非指导沙盘。
