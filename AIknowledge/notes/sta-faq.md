## Reading note

Sandplay can involve children or adults and allows expression through making scenes. The therapeutic relationship and the individual’s engagement matter; access to a tray and miniatures alone does not reproduce the whole therapeutic process.

沙盘可用于儿童或成人，允许通过制作场景表达。治疗关系与个人参与很重要；仅提供沙盘与微缩物不能复制完整治疗过程。
