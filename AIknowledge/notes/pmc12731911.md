## Reading note

Nonrandomized school group sandplay study of 101 children at risk for ADHD, with a comparison group. Group allocation and setting limit causal and general claims. Risk-group findings are not diagnostic rules for tray contents.

101名存在注意缺陷多动风险儿童的非随机学校团体沙盘对照研究。分组与场景限制因果及推广结论；风险组结果不是沙盘内容诊断规则。
