## Reading note

This review reports 27 studies across eight countries. Results are encouraging, but stronger research is still needed. Differences in design, participants and outcomes limit what can be concluded for a particular client.

该综述报告了八个国家的27项研究。结果具有潜力，但仍需更强研究。设计、参与者与结果指标差异限制了对具体来访者的推论。
