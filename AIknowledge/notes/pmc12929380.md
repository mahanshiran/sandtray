## Reading note

A narrative review included eight pediatric studies and found no eligible digital sand-therapy studies. Evidence about therapist-led physical sandplay cannot be assumed to validate digital sandtray or AI-generated interpretations.

该叙述综述纳入八项儿科研究，未发现符合条件的数字沙盘治疗研究。治疗师带领的实体沙盘证据不能直接证明数字沙盘或AI解释有效。
