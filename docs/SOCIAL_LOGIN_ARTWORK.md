# Social sign-in artwork

Bundled provider-supplied PNG button artwork (not downloaded at runtime):

- `Assets/Resources/SocialLogin/Google.png`: Light, icon-only square, Android/Web
  @4x, from https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/signin-assets.zip
  linked by https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines.
- `Assets/Resources/SocialLogin/Apple.png`: Apple logo-only button from
  https://appleid.cdn-apple.com/appleid/button/logo?color=black&border=false&border_radius=8&scale=4&size=48
  as documented at https://developer.apple.com/documentation/signinwithapple/incorporating-sign-in-with-apple-into-other-platforms.

Display both at 48 x 48 UI units, preserving the complete artwork and aspect ratio.
Provider marks remain owned by their respective companies and are used for sign-in.
